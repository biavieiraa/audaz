# 🌱 CultivAI

Project created for **NASA Space Apps Challenge**.

An agricultural assistant for **smallholder farmers**, designed to help with decisions on what to plant, when to plant, and how to care for the soil—all through simple, WhatsApp-style conversations.

🔗 **Access the project:** ([https://audaz-rosy.vercel.app/](https://audaz-rosy.vercel.app/))

> No installation required. Simply open the link on your mobile phone or computer.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Many smallholder farmers make planting decisions without easy access to climate data or technical guidance. This leads to crop losses and soil degradation.

## ✅ The Solution

CultivAI combines climate data with artificial intelligence in a simple, mobile-optimized interface, allowing farmers to receive practical, real-time recommendations.

## ✨ Features

- 💬 **Chatbot:** Get answers to your farming questions in plain, simple language.
- 🔄 **Crop Rotation:** Suggestions on what to plant next.
- 🌍 **Soil Conservation:** Tips for keeping your land healthy.
- 📅 **Calendar:** Organize your farming activities.
- 📊 **Dashboard:** An overview featuring local climate data.
- 📱 **Mobile-First:** An interface designed primarily for mobile devices.
